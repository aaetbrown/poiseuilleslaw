Circulation challenge

One thing goes wrong in an artery. Bring the blood flow back to normal with the heart’s push.

This is a simplificationThe model treats the artery as a rigid, evenly shaped tube. A real body has other ways to control blood flow, such as widening or narrowing small vessels, and real arteries pulse, branch, and have lumpy plaque. Here we are focusing on applying Poiseuille’s law. It is for learning physics, not for medical decisions..

What’s happening?

Goal: get the blood flow back to 53.9 mL/min using only the heart’s push. How close?Flow within 5% of normal counts as normal, which is about 51.2 to 56.6 mL/min.
